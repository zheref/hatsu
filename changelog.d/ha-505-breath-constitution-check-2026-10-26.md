### Added

- `hatsu:breath` § 4a: the plan-time constitution check [HR-10](docs/ROSTER.md#hr-10) rules, narrowed by [HR-337](docs/ROSTER.md#hr-337). It runs once per new effort, after the base tip is proved and before `rasengan` writes a line, over the request (the issue body, the brief or the maintainer's words, saved under the effort's `.nen/`). It sits inside `ren`'s step 1, never a seventh step ([HR-58](docs/ROSTER.md#hr-58)), and its record is the `breath` phase-ledger entry's `--note`: `constitution-check: read=…; not-binding=…; dangling=…; pass`, read back by `nen phase show` on a resume. A conflict with a binding clause is the new G5 row `constitution-conflict` in `nen/decisions.json`, carrying HR-10's two exits: **A** change the plan (starred) or **B** file the amendment. Nothing is authored before the answer. Closes #505.
- `scripts/constitution_check.mts` (lane `constitution-check`; gated into `unit-tests.yml` through `scripts/unit_tests_ci.mts`): the deterministic half of the check. It lists every HR and CON id the request cites, every binding HR row whose definition line names a skill the request names, and every row a `reversed by` / `superseded by` status points to. Each carries a binding of `whole`, `part` or `none`, read from `scripts/clause_id_guard.mts`'s status parser, which it imports. A dangling id exits `1`. Exit `2` covers a CON id with no `--constitution` (CON is read at the bankai-handbooks pin, [HR-338](docs/ROSTER.md#hr-338)), an unreadable input and a roster the clause guard refuses. The script is interim: zheref/nen#554 owes the verb to nen.
- `docs/WORKFLOW.md` § *The constitution check*: the read set, the CON source, the exits, the bounded judgement, the record, the stop and the resume. `docs/config/decisions.json.md` adds the row. `contracts/idle-permissions.json` lists the three commands the check runs.
