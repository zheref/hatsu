The table carries the row; § 4 takes over with nen's classes quoted. Hatsu declares no `mechanical` block, so its own catch-ups still stop at `1`.

Their dry runs read visibility, and `issue file --body-file` resolves a relative path against `--repo`.
  - `stage triage --nul`, `report data`'s `repo` and `issue edit-body --json`'s `v0.3` touch no Hatsu caller; the contract's `minimum_semantics` records each.
