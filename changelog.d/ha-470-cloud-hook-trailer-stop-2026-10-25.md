### Fixed

- **A hook that re-injects a forbidden trailer on every commit now stops the effort before its first push** ([#470](https://github.com/zheref/hatsu/issues/470)). A Cursor cloud agent's server-side `commit-msg` hook stamps `Co-authored-by` naming the person who started it on every commit, and a run on a consumer repository published such a commit and disclosed it, leaving a history rewrite only the maintainer may do. `kokusen` § 5 and `aka` §§ 4 and 7 now treat a second read-back of the same refused key as a re-injecting hook: drop the unpublished tip, keep the change, push nothing and stop at G5; publish-and-disclose stays only for a commit published before the run read it. `aka` § 7's pre-push guard now passes `--forbid-trailers-from <path>/nen/workflow.json`, so the `forbiddenTrailers` half of the read is mechanical and chained to `nen wc publish`.
- **`docs/AGENT-ATTRIBUTION.md` no longer claims a consumer has a required PR trailer check.** The check is `zheref/hatsu`'s own `plugin-bump-check` step; a consumer has one only where its own workflow runs it, Tenkai renders none yet and nen has no range verb for it. The doc now quotes Cursor's own documentation, fetched 2026-10-25: cloud agents add `Co-authored-by` to every commit, and no page documents a switch for it (the IDE/CLI *Attribution* toggle covers `Made with Cursor`). `docs/surfaces/cursor.md` carries the four quoted lines in its dated checklist (fourteen URLs now) and `docs/using/cursor.md` the caveat. Trims for `kokusen`'s ceiling: § 4's "Never `git add -A` blind" (§ 8 keeps the rule) and P5's "§ 8 still binds in full".
