### Changed

- **A blocker is captured before any wrap-up, and a failed launch-evidence read stops the launch (zheref/hatsu#508).** `docs/DISCOVERY.md` gains § *A blocker found while executing*: recover first within held authority, telling an unreviewed sandbox failure (retried once through the supported route) from an actual rejection (kept verbatim); capture through the four-pass reconciliation before any cleanup offer; filing that cannot complete is a `pending` record, never "filed"; uncertain ownership invents no evidence; a held run reports its objective, worktrees, checkpoint and resume condition and does not offer cleanup as the default. WORKFLOW § *Host workload budget* says a process-group read that fails before the launch stops the launch. `kazaana` § 7 and `futon` § 10 capture first, then ask the wrap-up picker. Eight bounded scenarios: `docs/ab/discovery-before-wrap-up.md`.
- **`build` § 2 shows an issue's shape miss in its first plan message and offers the amendment before authoring (zheref/hatsu#546);** declined or unanswered, the PR body states the gap.
- **`build` § 4 requires each linked phase skill to be loaded before its first command — `direct`, `ren`, `aka`, `mukai` with `shibari`, `en` — never typed from memory (zheref/hatsu#548).**
- **Runnable `nen pr ready` lines run through `scripts/gh_token_env.sh` (zheref/hatsu#550):** `en` § 5 item 2, `sharingan` § 3 step 1 and `izanami`'s readiness watch.
- **A PR GitHub closed on `base_ref_deleted` gets a successor, not an end (zheref/hatsu#495).** `en` § 6 gains the row — catch up, open a successor PR naming the one it replaces, a fresh durable slice, one comment on the old PR — and keeps *closed unmerged* for every other close; `mukai` step 8a and `ren` § 2a read the successor; WORKFLOW § *Stacked pull requests* rule 5 points to the row.
- **Delegated publication authority (HR-20) survives every handoff (zheref/hatsu#529).** `aka` § 0 names the five-fact record every handoff carries (invocation, any maintainer-approved prerequisite, repository, own branch, scope line), presented in a host approval request so a changed actor, worktree or commit needs no new human answer; WORKFLOW § *Delegate briefs* and `docs/surfaces/codex.md` § *Publication requests* carry it. Eight bounded cases: `docs/ab/publication-authority.md`.
- **Delivery provenance is checked when each hanten round returns, not first at publish (zheref/hatsu#542).** WORKFLOW § *The effort's ledgers* names the check — `nen report data` + `pr_delivery.ts normalize`, the session, phase and cycle ledgers — whose failure stops the run; a generated ledger is never hand-edited. `hanten` § 4 and `build` § 4 point to it.
