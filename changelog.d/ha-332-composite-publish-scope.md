### Changed

- **A typed `futon` or `kazaana` line now carries its working-branch publication scope through every handoff** ([#332](https://github.com/zheref/hatsu/issues/332)). `aka` § 0 gains a second carve-out: the typed invocation is the maintainer's one standing publish call for the branches that run authors — each effort's initial publication and every maintenance commit and push that delivers it, to the run's target repository and that effort's own branch — scoped by destination, never by commit SHA, and lapsing when the run ends. Another remote, branch, `branch.base`, a force form, a tag, a merge or a release is outside it and stands or falls on its owning skill's own rule (murasaki § 6, futon § 5's integration branch, getsuga § 3/§ 3a); an act no skill owns is not performed and is reported, and a G5 only where only the maintainer can do it. The scope lapses at the run's end, a halt or a G5 stop, and a resumed session holds it only on a fresh typed invocation. The reading is recorded in `docs/ROSTER.md` § *Rulings of 2026-10-19 — a typed composite's delegated publication* as this run's, the maintainer's to correct at the G4 merge. Every parent-to-builder and maintenance handoff carries the invocation verbatim with the repository and branch, and no later checkpoint narrows it to an exact commit. A host approval refusal stays the host's, resolved only through its supported approval path, with no wider grant and no alternative route. A representative trajectory (initial push, then a reviewed repair pushed to the same branch) is stated. `murasaki` § 6, `en`'s catch-up row, `mukai` step 6, `futon` § 7, `kazaana` § 3 and its Authority, and the `aka` row of `docs/STANDALONE-ENTRY.md` § 7 cite it; standalone `hatsu:aka` stays one call, one run. Plugin bumped to 0.118.0 and the three surface mirrors restamped.
