### Added

- `docs/WORKFLOW.md` § *Host workload budget — heavy local work*: the host workload budget, distinct from effort/worktree capacity (`nen loop slots --local-cap`) and remote-provider quotas. Light work is named (a read, git other than a push that starts CI on this host, `--dry-run`, a lint of the changed files, prose checks); anything else is heavy, including a push to a target whose CI runs on this host. The missing admission guard is owed to zheref/nen#536–#540, and every phase that runs a heavy verb reports `host admission: no guard (zheref/nen#536); serialized fallback` (rasengan, breath, tsukuyomi, kokusen, hanten's fixtures and reviewers, murasaki, kotoamatsukami, byakugan, susanoo, delegates). The serialized fallback: capacity fixed at one at this version; the heavy turn is held by the orchestrator, granted to one delegate at a time by a `Heavy turn: granted, workers <n>` / `Heavy turn: withheld` line, returned at a hand-back naming every recorded process group (a silent delegate with no known group is no recorded id, and the run parks) and re-granted in queue order; a reviewer always starts withheld and the orchestrator runs its pending heavy lanes; a pending lane is settled by a re-raise with the turn or by the orchestrator in that worktree; a delegate without the turn commits and publishes only where the push starts no same-host CI; finite workers, 2 unless the maintainer or a brief names another; same-host CI in two reads: a push is heavy only where its target declares a pool on this host, and before any heavy launch, whatever the target, every pool registered on this host is read through `nen runner inventory` (the set: `nen/repos.json` repositories whose `nen/workflow.json` `runners` carry this host's machine code, which is typed, never derived, HR-276). Busy is `parked:budget` at the minutes in an idle run and bounded waits then a G5 stop interactively; unknown (machine code, set or inventory) is `parked:g5-host-ci-unknown` idle and a G5 stop interactively. Overload is host-wide: in an idle run, `parked:g5-host-overload` and light-only for later efforts; interactively, a G5 stop. A retry needs a recorded process group (`ps -o pgid= -p $$` at launch; a group that differs from `$$` or cannot be read is no recorded id: an idle park, an interactive G5 stop) listed empty with `ps -A -o pid,ppid,pgid,command`, plus the maintainer's word on recovery. No stopped claim without that listing, no kill by name or pattern, and stopping any process, the session's own launch included, is the maintainer's G5. Closes #478.
