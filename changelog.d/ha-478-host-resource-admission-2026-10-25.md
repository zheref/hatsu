### Added

- `docs/WORKFLOW.md` § *Host workload budget — heavy local work*: the host workload budget, distinct from effort/worktree capacity (`nen loop slots --local-cap`) and remote-provider quotas. Light work is named (a read, git other than a push that starts CI on this host, `--dry-run`, a lint of the changed files, prose checks); anything else is heavy, including a push to a target whose CI runs on this host. The missing admission guard is owed to zheref/nen#536–#540, and every phase that runs a heavy verb reports `host admission: no guard (zheref/nen#536); serialized fallback` (rasengan, breath, kokusen, hanten's fixtures and reviewers, murasaki, kotoamatsukami, byakugan, susanoo, delegates).
