### Changed

- **`surface-mirror-check.yml`, `pr-readiness.yml` and `surface-mirror-regenerate.yml` hash the binary the bootstrap printed and compare it with the trusted checkout's `dependency.bootstrap.sha256["linux-x64"]`** ([#230](https://github.com/zheref/hatsu/issues/230)). Each fails closed on a mismatch, a missing or malformed key (a trailing newline included) or a missing contract, naming expected and observed. Nothing runs the binary before the match: the bootstrap step now prints only its path, and `nen --version` runs last in the check. The bootstrap script verifies the binary against `SHA256SUMS` from the same release, so a moved tag or replaced assets verified against themselves. The change landed in two steps: the validator learned the step and the bootstrap's next form first, then the live workflows took them and Hatsu's own forms were narrowed to carrying them, frozen (`NEN_DIGEST_FROZEN`, `NEN_BOOTSTRAP_NEXT`); in Hatsu's own repository tenkai's rendered readiness form is refused. The live step matches the published v0.21.0 linux-x64 binary and fails a one-byte tamper without running it. **A repin moves the linux-x64 digest with `pinned_ref`, in the same pull request,** or every pull request after it fails closed: `hatsu:ten` § 1, `docs/NEN-CONTRACT.md` and `docs/config/contract.json.md` say so, and `scripts/nen_global_fixture_check.sh` case 21c refuses a pin whose record was left behind. Tenkai's rendered readiness form carries no digest step yet, and the optional bootstrap-script digest is not recorded: both are owed, filed by the run's harvest. Whether this check is BC-11 bootstrap-class shell is an open G4 question, recorded in `docs/GATE-CONFIGURATION.md`. Plugin bumped to 0.105.0; mirrors restamped.
