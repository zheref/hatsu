### Changed

- **futon runs on nen's run manifest (zheref/hatsu#423).** `scripts/futon_effort_record.mts start` opens the manifest through `nen run start --id <run id> --target --repo [--idle-answers]` and only then writes its ownership record, contract `hatsu.futon.run/v0.3` (id, repo, invocation, selector, `startedAt`, `dry`, and the idle profile's `terminal`, `budget`, `source`; no PRs, no parks, no answers). Every opened PR is `nen run append`, every park `nen run park`, the `then` gate and the register `nen run verify`, the budget `nen run budget --max-minutes --max-tokens` with the committed profile's values, and futon § 6's slots `nen loop slots --from-run <run id> --target` — no typed efforts file. The record's `append`, `park` and `status` are retired and refuse by name; a new `profile` prints the ownership record. A run started before this release has no nen manifest, so its `then` gate is held. Park rows are `[a-z0-9-]+`: `g5-<class>` and `residue-<skill>` replace the colon forms nen refuses.
- **`--idle` is nen's to read.** `nen parse futon` reads the token (nen `0.26.0`); the record no longer strips it, and still refuses a doubled `--idle`, a modifier after `then` and any unknown `--` token before it, which nen would fold into the label.
- **The issue shape is nen's (HR-4).** `hatsu:build` § 2, `hatsu:futon` § 2, `hatsu:file` § 4 and `hatsu:emperor-time` § 6 run `nen issue questions` and `nen issue body-check --requirements-from "$hatsu_root/contracts/issue-sections.json"`, stated once in `docs/config/plugin-registries.md` § *The check*. `scripts/issue_body_check.mts` and its fixture are retired; the `issue-shape` lane now runs `scripts/issue_sections_fixture.mts`, which holds the templates to the catalogue and runs the verified nen over them. A vague adjective or technology term on an acceptance row is now an advisory, never a miss.

### Added

- **`hatsu:futon <repo>@<selector> --idle --dry-run` (zheref/hatsu#424).** The idle profile's rehearsal in one typed line: every read-only verb runs, nothing is authored, every effort's dry end state is parked by its row (`dry-prompt` inside the local cap, `dry-waiting-slot` past it, or the stop the read predicts), and the record says `dry: true`. The record lifts `--dry-run` off before nen's parse until nen reads it; without `--idle` it is refused. `docs/ab/futon.md` § 2026-10-23 is the proof: 25 issues, 7 authorable in one night. This release is `0.140.0`.
- **The maintainer's ruling of 2026-10-23 on idle runs** (`R-2026-10-23-01`, quoted in WORKFLOW § 2 `idle`): unattended except an act that may not be reversed. Every ask-once row that stars an option now resolves `default` (`harness-attribution-switch`, `host-nen-link`, `cleanup-plan` as this session's deterministic rows, the starred `spec-*` rows); rows that star none hold; `publish-offer` stays `refuse`. The floor is `publish-offer` refuse and `missing-maintainer-choice` at most hold. An idle run always takes the integration-branch shape and never merges into `main`. Every decision taken for the maintainer is listed in the new PR-body footer section **Decisions taken on your behalf** (`decisions`, resolved with `pr_sections_resolve.sh --idle`), in the final brief and in the register's `legendRows`.
- **Review settlement.** futon § 6 sums two slot counts: delivery PRs through `--from-run`, and claimed efforts with no PR through `--efforts` (HR-257). `nen run budget`'s four exit rows are stated. The record copies nen's `startedAt`, refuses a manifest that stores other answers than it sent, and names nen's manifest as the orphan when its own write fails. `bind` refuses a dry run. A dry run's predictions go on the record through a new `predict` verb, never into nen's `parked[]`. futon § 1 lifts `--dry-run` before its echo parse, and § 7 holds every act under `--dry-run`.
- **The maintainer's second ruling of 2026-10-23** (`R-2026-10-23-02`, quoted in WORKFLOW § *Idle batches* rule 9): nothing blocks a batch. An issue with an open `OQ-{n}` row, or whose questions cannot be read, is skipped (`nen run park --row needs-clarification` / `g5-shape-unread`) and the queue continues. futon § 10's closing report and the Rikugan register list every skipped issue with its open rows, and the report ends by offering (never starting) the open-question flow: one picker per open row, each answer recorded through `nen stop answer --record` and struck into the issue through `nen issue edit-body --expect-body-sha256`, then read back with `nen issue questions`.
- **Local slots free at `ready+prompted` (zheref/hatsu#425).** The maintainer's pick (`R-2026-10-23-20`) amends HR-257's *frees at its local merge* and reconciles the 2026-10-13 stacked-slot rule. Every effort, stacked or not, frees its local slot once its PR is Ready and its merge prompt (or, for a stacked PR, its Ready stop) was shown. A claimed effort with no PR holds its slot until then, or until its local merge into the integration branch. `monitor.localSlotFrees` and the catalogue default are `ready+prompted`, and the idle `start` refuses `merged` and `stacked-open`, here or under `idle.localSlotFrees`. futon § 11's cap is now 7 local slots, not 7 worktrees.
