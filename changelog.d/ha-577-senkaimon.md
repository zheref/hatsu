### Added

- **`hatsu:senkaimon` — a consumer's open PRs tried one head at a time on a device** (zheref/hatsu#577). `hatsu:senkaimon <repo> [--target <name>]` shows the open-PR queue first, ordered by number, with every fork head and every head that changes `nen/contract.json` or `nen/workflow.json` listed ineligible with its reason and every other head's changed paths shown — read from objects before any checkout, so a same-repo head is trusted (SEC-16) but never presented as vetted; takes one authorization (new row `review-queue-authorize`) for the declared target and the core swap and launch; then per PR checks its pinned head out hook-less into a review worktree (`.nen/worktrees/senkaimon-<surface>`, named in WORKFLOW § *Where worktrees live*), view-swaps it into core, reads core's HEAD back and launches it through `amaterasu` § 4, waiting for a running launch to release core and the target. Each tested head gets one unstarred merge-or-revise call (new row `review-verdict`) naming the PR, the tested head and the actual target: the merge path re-reads the head and runs `en` § 5's five checks as they stand with that head pinned (`nen pr ready --require-head`), its prompt offered, never run; the revision path hands the typed feedback to a `ren` effort on the PR's branch. However the run ends, `nen wc swap --return` puts core back with its parked work restored. The name is the maintainer's pick (HR-388, record `R-2026-11-10-01`). Its read-only probes and the SC-1 proof protocol it still owes are `docs/ab/senkaimon.md`.
- **`scripts/gh_api_read.mts pr-head <owner/repo> <N>`** — one fixed GET projecting a PR's head SHA, head and base repositories, state and draft flag, so senkaimon can tell a fork head without raw `gh`; the `gh-api-read-guard` fixture pins the exact argv, projection included, and refuses a hostile number or flag.
