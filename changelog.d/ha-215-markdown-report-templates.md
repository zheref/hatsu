### Changed

- **Every report now has a Markdown template beside its HTML one, filled by `nen report render` from the same data document** ([#215](https://github.com/zheref/hatsu/issues/215); its predecessor [#145](https://github.com/zheref/hatsu/issues/145) fixed where the artifact goes). `templates/spiritual-message.md` covers `turn`, `turn-fast` and `landing`; `templates/rikugan.md` covers `final` and `register`. Each mirrors its HTML template block for block, from the same `reports.sections.<variant>.blocks`: one starred option per ask, the `nen pr ready` verdict quoted, the G5 evidence in `g5-blocker`, and the delta as a `mermaid` fence. On Antigravity the artifact is no longer composed from a description, so its structure no longer drifts from turn to turn: it is rendered to `<reports.dir>/current.md` (nen's `--out` stays inside `--repo`), copied verbatim into `write_to_file`, and read back with `cmp`. `docs/PROCESS.md` § *The Markdown report* states the path, the one step no verb performs, the fixed structure and the escaping rules; `spiritual-message` § 7, `rikugan` §§ 4–5, `backlog-board` § 3 and rules item 9 point at it; `docs/surfaces/antigravity.md` records the render path and its provenance. A new `report-markdown-guard` lane renders every variant in both formats from `templates/report-examples/` and holds the block order, the desk rules, GFM-safe escaping and the checked examples (29 fixture cases). Owed to nen: render to stdout or to a path outside `--repo`, which would retire the copy step. Plugin bumped to 0.102.0; mirrors regenerated at that stamp.
