### Fixed

- **A placed copy is Hatsu's own only where its marker is generated** (zheref/hatsu#531, `SEC-7`; Feitan's F5 on the pull request closing zheref/hatsu#530). The predicates that authorise the warm-up's unattended full-surface install, and decide which placed files a refresh may replace or remove, asked whether a marker's text appeared anywhere in a file's bytes, so a page that only quoted a concrete marker passed. They now share one reader, `scripts/surface_marker.sh` (POSIX sh and awk, sourced by `scripts/surface_bootstrap.sh`, `scripts/permissions_pack.sh` and `hooks/session-start.sh` with its three mirrors), which reads the one line nen writes the marker on: the first markdown line after any frontmatter, a hook script's line 2, a JSON file's line 2. It accepts the generated form alone, in the current and the pre-#530 stamped grammar, plus the retired Antigravity generator's marker where that generator wrote it. The surface name is compared with `=`, never matched, so `codex-experimental` is not `codex`. The retired Antigravity marker no longer counts as a hook script's ownership on codex or cursor. A verified plugin root without the reader refreshes nothing and names the file. The reader is one awk run per file, with no subshell of the caller and silent under a caller's `set -x`: per-file trace records from it broke the Bashcov capture of `surface-bootstrap-guard` on every run until they were removed. Before and after over the 258 generated files under `surfaces/`, every file keeps its verdict under every reader.

### Added

- **A mirrored source carrying a concrete marker literal is refused before it is checked or generated** (zheref/hatsu#531, AC-3). `scripts/surface_mirror_check.sh` and `scripts/surface_mirror_regenerate.sh` refuse by file and line, at exit 1, a skill, persona or include, `hooks/hooks.json` or a hook script, `claude/rules/hatsu.md`, `contracts/permissions.json` or `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` that carries `GENERATED by nen surface mirror (surface: <name>` with a concrete name, or the retired Antigravity marker. An older session-start hook still placed in a consumer's workspace matches the text anywhere in a file. Prose writes a placeholder, `(surface: <s>)`.

### Changed

- The fixtures write every marker in its generated form and position, and cover the new reader: its grammar rows under bash and a POSIX sh (`surface-bootstrap-guard`); a quoted mention that is neither adoption nor ownership; a foreign marker under the shared `.agents/`; a genuine stamped marker that is still adoption, at the bootstrap and at the hook; a quoted or foreign `.cursor/cli.json` kept by the permission pack (`permissions-pack-guard`); a root without the reader (`nen-global-guard`); and the source refusal on every source kind (`surface-mirror-guard`). `docs/using/mirrors.md` states the rule and corrects where a JSON mirror carries its marker.
