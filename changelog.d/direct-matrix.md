### Added

- **`docs/DIRECT-MATRIX.md` — the model matrix, readable without running `hatsu:direct`.** One page answers which model, surface and effort `direct` will recommend for any work. It has the ten aliases with their provider, family, surface, the name you type and the dated snapshot model; a grid of all 39 jobs × 5 domains naming each winner, with per-language winners inline and fallback routes marked; per-domain tables with runner-ups, language differences and interactive equivalents; the domain rules, the effort rule with job weights and each surface's dial; the aggregation rule; and six worked examples with their restart lines.
  - **Rendered, never hand-written**: `scripts/direct_matrix_doc.sh` builds the page from `contracts/direct.registry.json`, `contracts/classify.taxonomy.json` and `nen/workflow.json` → `models`, the files `nen direct resolve` reads. `--check` fails when the page is stale and is seated as the new focused lane `direct-matrix-guard` in `nen/contract.json`. `--verify` asks `nen direct resolve` for every rendered cell, fallback and worked example (961 checks at nen `v0.20.0`, all agreeing).
  - Linked from the README's introduction and from `docs/WORKFLOW.md` § *`direct` — the model, surface and effort*.
