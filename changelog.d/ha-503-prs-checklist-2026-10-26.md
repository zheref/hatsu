- **The reviewer-answered prose checklist (zheref/hatsu#503; SDD plan wave 4b, HR-369).** A new conditional footer block, `## 🔎 Prose checklist`, carries the four `PRS` rows the maintainer ruled: `PRS-C` criteria (every closing issue has AC rows in Given / When / Then), `PRS-L` measurable (each row names an observable or a number with its unit), `PRS-S` scope (the body's scope boundaries name what is out) and `PRS-M` markers (no open OQ row, every assumption an AS row). A `no` is also a Hanten finding with rule `PRS-<letter>`, settled like any other. `docs/PR-SECTIONS.md` § *The prose checklist* holds the rows; `claude/agents/chrollo.md` reads them.
