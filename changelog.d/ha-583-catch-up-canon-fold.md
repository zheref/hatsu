### Changed

- **A catch-up that resolved a conflict of meaning reopens review of the merged head** (zheref/hatsu#583). `en` § 3 now says the merged head is a changed tree for review as well as for tests: before the readiness verdict reaches the maintainer, `hanten` runs each applicable reviewer's bounded delta pass from its last reviewed head to the merge commit, under the effort's remaining budget, ahead of kokusen, kotoamatsukami and byakugan. An earlier settled round carries over its dispositions on paths the merge left byte-identical, never the resolved hunks, a finding whose cited lines moved, or its verdict as a claim about the merged head. Skipping the round is the maintainer's ruling only, and where they rule disclosure enough the merge prompt carries one fixed line. `en` § 5's waiver condition reads hanten as settled on the judged head; `ao` § 6 and `murasaki` § 4 point to the rule.
- **An unavailable host merge has a named route** (zheref/hatsu#586). `murasaki` § 4 derives the incoming diff (`git diff --name-only HEAD...origin/<base>`), compares it with the host's quoted refusal, takes ao's ordinary `nen wc catch-up` when no incoming path is protected and stops at G5 naming each protected path otherwise; one report line quoting the refusal satisfies its "tell the maintainer" and stops nothing on its own. The report names the route and its evidence. `en` § 3's catch-up step cites it. The protected set is the host's and not in the checkout, so the check stays a recorded judgement by the maintainer's ruling [HR-393](docs/ROSTER.md#hr-393); ROSTER 1.82.0.

Steps 4, 5 and 10 pass `--stack-dir handbooks/stacks --leaf rules`, nen resolving and verifying the canon checkout at the pin and naming how (`nen canon checkout`); the `mkdir` before `--markdown-out` is gone.
