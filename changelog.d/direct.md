- **`hatsu:direct`** (new, `claude/skills/direct/SKILL.md`; the second skill from the maintainer's rulings of 2026-10-04, stacked on v0.88.0's `classify`): from an issue's `lang/` and `job/` labels (or an inline effort classified on the spot), resolves deterministically — `nen direct resolve` over the new `contracts/direct.registry.json` (`hatsu.direct-registry/v1`) — and reports, for the winner and the runner-up: **Provider · Model family · Latest recommended version · Recommended surface · Recommended effort**, each with its **interactive equivalents**.
  - **Stable aliases, replaceable versions.** The registry's ten aliases (`SEMANTIC_FRONTIER`, `SEMANTIC_MAX`, `BALANCED_AUTHOR`, `EXECUTION_FRONTIER`, `EXECUTION_VALUE`, `VISUAL_ORCHESTRATOR`, `VISUAL_FALLBACK`, `MECHANICAL_EDITOR`, `PR_QUALITY_REVIEW`, `PR_SECURITY_REVIEW`) each name a provider, a model family, their Hatsu surface and the `nen/workflow.json` → `models.<surface>` tier the consumer spells it as. No version is in an alias or a routing cell: the latest is read live (the surface's own lookup, then the provider's model page, quoted with its date, never inside a command), with the document's dated `snapshot` as the stated fallback.
  - **95 job × domain routes (204 cells with their language overrides)**, transcribed from the document's five tables (the document's digest is in `source.documentSha256`); a cell's surface is always its alias's (`surfaceRule`), so the actionable winner and runner-up name one of Hatsu's four surfaces — or a review product beside its actionable `also` alias — and the document's Cursor and native-IDE preferences, with GitHub Copilot, are the interactive rows. The aggregation of several pairs, the empty-axis answer (`undirectable`) and the structured `domains.rule` are stated in the file.
  - **Effort is a rule**: the highest job weight plus one for each of three or more jobs, two or more code languages, the `aigov` domain — `low | medium | high | max`, mapped onto each surface's own dial (a collapsed top on Cursor and Antigravity reads `high`, said).
  - **Persistence is local**: `.nen/direct/<effort>.json`, keyed by the issue identity (`<CODE>-IS-#<N>`), known before any branch exists; no issue comment, no label.
