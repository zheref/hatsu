### Fixed

- **The macOS in-session bell plays its sound and can no longer hang (zheref/hatsu#516).** `scripts/bell_notify.sh` passed `afplay -- <file>`, an option the player rejects, so the sound rung never played and the error was reported as a missing seat; the sound is an absolute allowlisted `/System/Library/Sounds` path, so the player now takes it bare. The OS notification waited synchronously with no deadline, and a hung `osascript` blocked the turn for more than five minutes and the sound rung behind it. Each rung now runs as the script's own background child under a documented finite deadline, `--deadline <seconds>` (1 to 60, default 5), with no GNU `timeout`: past it the child gets TERM, then KILL a second later, by its PID only, never by name or pattern, and is reaped before the sound rung runs once. The result lines now tell an absent tool (`not applicable`) from one that ran and reported an error (`failed (exit <n>: <stderr>)`) and one that never returned (`timed out after <n>s, its own child stopped`), and none of them reads `sent` or `played`; `sent` still never confirms delivery. The `bell-guard` fixture adds deterministic success, unavailable, failure, hung-child and TERM-ignoring cases, a same-named decoy that must survive, and the bound measured. `docs/surfaces/README.md` § *Shared rules* says the same. The Stop hook had the same hang: `hooks/stop-bell.sh` waited on `osascript` with no deadline. On macOS and Linux each of its rung tools now runs as the hook's own child under `HATSU_BELL_DEADLINE` (whole seconds, 1 to 60, default 5; any other value reads 5). A watchdog, also the hook's own child, TERMs and then KILLs that one PID, and the sound still plays once after a timed-out notification; Windows is unchanged and still untested. `scripts/stop_bell_fixture.mts` proves hung and TERM-ignoring notifiers return within the bound with a same-shaped decoy untouched. Out of scope: the Notification Center/XPC seat error itself. Plugin 0.158.1, mirrors regenerated.
