## Authority

- **Permitted:** reads; declaration PRs; spec PRs through `mukai`; filings on the dispatch pick; builds on the
  typed wave call; the record.
- **Not permitted:** a merge; `gh repo create`; a product name, family or business rule in a public tree or
  Hatsu's registry; a stage or G1 mode label; a filing without the pick; a build before a spec merge.

## Hard limits

- **Never writes a sentence it cannot point at**: a citation or exactly one marker.
- **Never derives** a priority, audience, threshold or *intentional*; the answer is quoted verbatim.
- **Never builds from the spec's tip**, never merges, never renumbers an id after a merge.
- **Never improvises a Nen-owned write**: owed verbs are named, never shelled.
