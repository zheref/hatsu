# Kamui — the build that leaves from a tree nobody has open

**Nature: Emitter**, carrying **Transmuter** for § 3's worktree; § 4's steps hold their own natures.

> **Cut me a clean tree at the trunk's tip, put my own files in it, build there, and send that —
> never whatever sits in my checkout.**

**Ruling 2026-09-29 (HA#146), quoted**: *"Great to collapse Step #1 and #2 if fully idempotent."* —
*"Let's have the composite ALWAYS copy own file from core checkout."* The evidence: a
`$kagutsuchi testflight` from a fresh worktree found no archive there and one in core — built
nine days earlier, **95 commits behind `origin/main`**, on a dirty tree — and every plan row read `ok`.
Plugin root and the declaration gate by role: [PROCESS.md](../../../docs/PROCESS.md) § *Authority
every phase shares*. **The phases this composite calls skip their own `## 0.`** and say kamui holds
them ([`STANDALONE-ENTRY.md`](../../../docs/STANDALONE-ENTRY.md) § 4).

target or run. A [`futon`](../futon/SKILL.md) `then kamui[@<target>]` step is the **advance go**
[`mugetsu`](../mugetsu/SKILL.md) § 3 defines, gated by `futon.advanceGo` (`kamui` listed beside

## 3. The worktree — steps one and two collapsed, idempotent

**Core** is `nen wc worktrees --repo <any checkout> --json`'s `core`, quoted in the report; then
`nen wc swap --status --repo <core>` — **an active swap refuses**, naming
[`amenotejikara`](../amenotejikara/SKILL.md) `return`, and a status that does not answer is refused too
([`amaterasu`](../amaterasu/SKILL.md) § 3's rule).

```sh
"$hatsu_root/scripts/kamui_worktree.sh" --repo <path> [--base <branch.base>] --dry-run   # the plan, first
"$hatsu_root/scripts/kamui_worktree.sh" --repo <path> [--base <branch.base>]
```

The script's header is the contract; what it does, in order: **fetches `origin/<base>`**; keeps **one
detached worktree at `<core>/.nen/worktrees/kamui`** — absent → `created`; present, clean, at the tip
→ `reused` (**a re-run at the tip is a no-op, not a second worktree**); present, clean, elsewhere →
`moved`; **dirty → exit `2` listing every path, never discarded**; a directory there that is not a
worktree → refused, somebody's; a registration whose directory vanished → pruned, then created. Then
**every `project.fromCore` path is copied from core** — refused, exit `2`, when it is tracked, not
gitignored, absent in core, a symlink, or leaves the tree — and the report carries one `copied:` line
per path. **Always from core, never from another checkout, never synthesised.**

**Invoked from core, the worktree is still where § 4 runs**: say *invoked from core: building in
`<worktree>`, not here*. **Never `nen shu archive` or `nen shu deploy` in core.** The `--dry-run`
fetches nothing and says *as last fetched*; the bare run is the one that moves anything.

## 4. Build, then send — in the worktree, each under its own skill

1. **[`$susanoo`](../susanoo/SKILL.md)**, `--repo <worktree>`, its `## 0.` skipped: the archive
   lands in the worktree's own `.nen/`, and its P2 line reads the tip and a clean tree by
   construction. Its stops are its own — a seat (exit `4`) quoted, an unsupported host (`3`) or a
   signing refusal (`2`/`5`) a **G5** — and **nothing is handed onward from a failed or partial
   archive.**
2. **[`$kagutsuchi`](../kagutsuchi/SKILL.md) `[<target>]`**, `--repo <worktree>`, its `## 0.`
   skipped: the plan, **§ 3a's freshness gate run and quoted — fresh by construction is never fresh by
   inference** — the send on § 1's call, § 4a's tag where declared. Its exit table is its own.

**The authority for `--run` is the maintainer's typed call in § 1, carried whole** — this composite
adds none, and a run that reaches step 2 without that call prints the plan, says it has none, and
stops, exactly as kagutsuchi § 1 says. **One call, one target, one send.**

## Residue

`git worktree add --detach`, `git checkout --detach`, `git worktree prune`, `git check-ignore` and
`cp -p` are the script's raw calls — **nen has no worktree verb** (an owned dependency for nen; `nen wc
worktrees` reads, never writes). The per-stack `deploy.defaultTarget` in `nen shu detect`'s reference
pack is nen's to add. The worktree lives under core's gitignored `.nen/`, so a consumer's `.gitignore`
that does not ignore `.nen/` would show it as untracked — reported, never added to `.gitignore` here.
Verified live against zheref/KroApple in [`docs/ab/kamui.md`](../../../docs/ab/kamui.md).

## Authority and hard limits

- **Permitted, only on the maintainer's own call:** fetch; one detached worktree at the fixed path;
  the declared `fromCore` copies from core; susanoo and kagutsuchi **in that worktree**, kagutsuchi's
  send and § 4a tag on that same call.
- **Not permitted:** `nen shu archive` or `nen shu deploy` in core; a second worktree; `--discard`,
  `reset --hard` or `clean` on a dirty worktree; a copy from any checkout but core, or a synthesised
  one; a target neither typed nor declared; production or a store (`mugetsu` at **G3**); `nen shu
  release`; a commit, push, PR, label or merge; any tag other than kagutsuchi § 4a's.
- **Never runs unasked or proposes itself**; never treats a declaration, a delegation or a green
  archive as the call; never carries the call to another target or another run.
- **Never infers freshness** — kagutsuchi § 3a runs in the worktree and is quoted — and never sends
  what § 4 step 1 did not build **in this run**.
- **Never edits a declaration to make a copy or a send work**: a missing `fromCore` row or target is
  a declaration PR at that repository's gate (**G4** canon, **G2** consumer).
